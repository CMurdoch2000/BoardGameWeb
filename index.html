Rolling the Dice One Last Time

It is with both sadness and gratitude that we announce the closure of Top Hat and Bishop. Our doors will officially close on the 1st of September. We are deeply thankful to everyone who has supported us, visited us, and helped make this space such a welcoming and lively community hub.

From the clatter of dice and the shuffle of cards to the glow of arcade screens and the sound of laughter filling the room, Top Hat and Bishop has been more than just a café, it has been a place for people to come together. We’ve seen friendships spark over board games, families share moments of connection, and countless evenings made brighter by those who chose to spend them here.

We want to sincerely thank every single person who stepped inside, grabbed a drink and pulled up a chair. Whether you came once or many times, you’ve helped shape the spirit of Top Hat and Bishop. While our doors will close on the 1st of September, the memories made here will live on, and we hope you carry a piece of Top Hat and Bishop with you into all your future game nights.

Thank you for letting us be part of your story, and for being such an important part of ours.
