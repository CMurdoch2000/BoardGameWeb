Arcade Games

Come down and play some of our great retro arcade machines! Go head to head on Outrun 2 or Tekken, team up on Ghost Squad, get nostalgic with 80s classics like Space Invaders or Pacman or try our huge range of console games!

Ghost Squad

  • Ghost Squad is an action-packed arcade shooter that immerses players in the role of elite operatives on high-stakes missions. Released in 2004, the game features light gun gameplay, allowing players to aim and shoot at enemies in various scenarios, such as hostage rescues and bomb defusals. With multiple pathways and replayability, Ghost Squad offers a thrilling and intense experience for action game fans.

OutRun 2

  • OutRun 2, released in 2003, is an exhilarating arcade racing game that captures the thrill of high-speed driving. Players race a Ferrari through stunning, diverse environments, from sunny beaches to bustling cities. With its smooth graphics, responsive controls, and a catchy soundtrack, the game offers a branching course system that keeps gameplay fresh, making it a beloved title among racing enthusiasts.

Pac-Man

  • Pac-Man, released in 1980, is a groundbreaking arcade game that revolutionized gaming with its simple yet addictive gameplay. Players control Pac-Man as he navigates a maze, eating pellets while avoiding colorful ghosts. Power pellets allow him to turn the tables and eat the ghosts for bonus points. Its iconic design and catchy music have made Pac-Man a cultural phenomenon and enduring classic.

Tekken 3

  • Tekken 3, released in 1997, is a landmark fighting game that transformed the genre with its fluid 3D graphics and dynamic gameplay. Introducing characters like Jin Kazama and Hwoarang, it features diverse fighting styles and intricate combos. The game’s innovative mechanics, including sidestepping and a robust roster, made it a beloved classic and a defining title in the Tekken series.

Mario Kart Wii

  • Mario Kart Wii, released in 2008, revitalized the beloved racing series with its vibrant tracks and iconic characters. Featuring motion controls with the Wii Wheel, players raced as favorites like Mario and Luigi, navigating unique courses and using power-ups to gain an edge. With multiplayer modes and online play, it quickly became a fan favorite and a standout title on the Wii.

Raspberry Pi

  • The Raspberry Pi is a powerful yet compact computer that enables users to easily emulate a wide range of classic video games from various consoles. By running lightweight operating systems, the Raspberry Pi can simulate hardware from systems such as the NES, SNES, Sega Genesis, and more, effectively transforming it into a versatile retro gaming machine.
