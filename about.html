About

Glasgow's newest board game cafe!

Freshly opened on Sauchiehall Street, Top Hat and Bishop combines new and traditional board games and retro arcade games with locally roasted coffee and home made cakes and snacks, including games such as:

  • Mysterium
  • Cluedo
  • Sherlock Holmes 221B Baker St
  • Ticket to Ride
  • Dixit
  • Sleeping Gods
  • Gloomhaven
  • Above and Below

We will be also be hosting events and clubs in the evenings, for any booking enquires or to simply learn more about us or our events, contact us at: hello@tophatandbishop.co.uk
