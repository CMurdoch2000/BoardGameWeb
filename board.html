Game Types

Mystery

Mystery board games require you to use your deductive skills to crack puzzles and solve mysteries. These can be a fun way to spend time with your family or a group of friends while collaborating on tasks or trying to outwit your opponents.

  • Mysterium
  • Cluedo
  • Deception
  • Sherlock Holmes 221B Baker Street
  • Exit Series
  • Cluedo Mansion

Storytelling

Storytelling games involve players creating a story based on the game's theme or prompt, usually with the help of game components like cards or dice. They often have a set of rules or mechanics that provide a structure for the storytelling, such as assigning characters or settings to each player.

  • Dixit
  • Sleeping Gods
  • Gloomhaven
  • Above and Below
  • Oath

Strategy

Strategy games involve players making decisions and planning moves in order to achieve a specific goal or objective. These games typically have a complex set of rules and mechanics, and require players to think critically and strategically in order to succeed.

  • Splendor
  • Carcassone
  • Ticket to Ride
  • Forbidden Island
  • Dune
  • Catan
  • Orleans
  • Imhotep
  • ...Plus many more in store!

Card Games

Card games can be enjoyed by everyone, there are many different types of card games, but most involve players using the cards in their hand to win points or achieve a specific objective.

  • Bonanza
  • Uno
  • Saboteur
  • Jaipur
  • Cards Against Humanity
  • Munchkin Deluxe

Family

Family games are designed to be played by family members (or friends) of all ages and skill levels. These games are typically easy to learn and play, and offer a mix of strategy, luck, and social interaction.

  • Monopoly
  • Risk
  • Hungry Hippos
  • King of Tokyo
  • Castle Panic
  • Gear & Piston
  • Connect 4

Classic

These games have been played for centuries, and their rules and gameplay have remained largely unchanged over the years. Many of these games have become cultural icons and are still enjoyed by people all around the world.

  • Chess
  • Draughts
  • Go
  • Dominoes
  • Backgammon
  • Mahjong
